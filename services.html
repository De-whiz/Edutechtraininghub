Our Services

EdTech Training Hub is more than a course provider. We help individuals, schools, organisations and businesses design, build and deliver effective digital learning experiences.

Seven ways we help you learn, teach and build

Each service is practical, hands-on and tailored to where you are and where you want to go.

Service 01

Instructional Design

We help individuals, schools, organisations and businesses transform their knowledge into structured and engaging learning experiences. Our services may include:

  • Course design
  • Curriculum development
  • Learning experience design
  • Storyboarding
  • eLearning content development
  • Assessment design
Service 02

LMS Integration & Support

We help schools, organisations, trainers and course creators set up and use Learning Management Systems to deliver structured online learning. Services include:

  • LMS consultation
  • LMS setup and integration
  • Course organisation
  • Learning content implementation
  • LMS support and guidance
Service 03

Online Course Development

Have knowledge you want to turn into a course? We help you move from idea to structured learning experience. From course structure and learning objectives to content organisation and digital resources, we create a course that is clear, engaging and learner-focused.

Perfect for anyone who wants to package their expertise into a digital product.
Service 04

Digital Skills Training

We provide practical digital skills training for educators, schools, organisations, teams, professionals and entrepreneurs. Training can cover digital productivity, AI tools, online teaching, content creation and other relevant digital skills.

  • Digital productivity
  • AI tools
  • Online teaching
  • Content creation
Service 05

Teacher & Educator Professional Development

We help educators develop the digital and instructional skills needed to teach effectively in modern learning environments.

Designed for teachers and educators who want to grow with confidence in the digital age.
Service 06

Online Tutoring & Academic Support

We provide personalised online academic support for learners, with a focus on understanding, confidence and strong foundational skills. Learning is tailored to the learner’s needs rather than relying solely on their grade level.

  • Personalised 1:1 support
  • Strengthen foundational knowledge
  • Build understanding & confidence
Service 07

Digital Learning & EdTech Consultation

We help organisations and education professionals identify appropriate technology and learning solutions for their needs. This can include:

  • EdTech strategy
  • Digital learning implementation
  • Technology selection
  • Online learning workflows
  • Digital transformation support

Not sure which service fits your needs?

Tell us about your goals and we’ll recommend the right learning solution for you, your school or your organisation.
