## 5. ABOUT US PAGE

### Page intro
- **Title:** About EdTech Training Hub
- **Tagline:** Empowering People to Learn, Teach, Create and Thrive in the Digital Age

### Who We Are (Overview)
EdTech Training Hub Ltd is a digital learning and professional development company focused on helping educators, knowledge professionals, coaches, entrepreneurs, organisations and learners thrive in an increasingly digital world.

We bridge the gap between traditional learning and technology by providing practical digital skills training, online teaching support, instructional design, eLearning solutions, LMS integration and personalised learning opportunities.

We help people not only learn how to use technology, but understand how to use it meaningfully to teach, create, design, share knowledge and build opportunities. Through our training programmes and EdTech services, we equip professionals with the skills and tools needed to create engaging digital learning experiences while also supporting learners through personalised and technology-enabled education.

### Our Vision
To transform learning and professional development by making technology-enabled education accessible, practical and impactful for everyone.

We envision a world where educators, professionals, organisations and learners have the digital skills, tools and confidence they need to learn, teach and create meaningful opportunities in the digital age.

### Our Mission
To empower educators, knowledge professionals, organisations and learners with the digital skills, technology and learning solutions they need to succeed. We achieve this by providing:
- Practical digital skills training
- Online teaching and tutoring development
- Instructional design and eLearning solutions
- LMS integration and support
- AI and EdTech training
- Digital product and course development support
- Professional development opportunities
- Personalised learning experiences for students

### What Makes Us Different
We don't believe technology should replace good teaching. We believe technology should make good teaching **better**.

Our approach combines: **Education + Technology + Learning Design + Practical Skills**

Rather than simply teaching people how to use tools, we focus on helping them understand when, why and how to use those tools effectively. Our programmes are practical and hands-on — participants don't just watch lessons, they apply what they learn, create projects and build evidence of their skills. We also recognise that people don't start from the same place, so our programmes help beginners build confidence while allowing more experienced participants to continue developing their skills.

### Our Target Audience
- **Educators & Teachers** — develop digital skills, teach online and create engaging learning experiences
- **Online Tutors** — improve online teaching skills, attract learners and build sustainable tutoring opportunities
- **Trainers & Facilitators** — deliver workshops, training programmes and professional development
- **Coaches & Consultants** — package knowledge into coaching programmes, training or digital learning experiences
- **Knowledge Professionals & Subject-Matter Experts** — teach, create digital products or build online learning experiences
- **Instructional Designers & eLearning Professionals** — strengthen learning design, digital learning and EdTech skills
- **Schools & Education Organisations** — digital transformation, teacher training, LMS implementation and learning technology support
- **Entrepreneurs & Creators** — turn knowledge, skills or experience into digital products, courses or online services
- **Students & Learners** — personalised academic support and strong foundational learning

### Values strip
- Education-first approach
- Practical, hands-on learning
- Technology as an enabler
- Inclusive for all skill levels
